From now on, whenever we add a feature, let's ask these three questions:

Can another module reuse this?
Example: the Media Library shouldn't be only for Studio.
Can this eventually be automated?
Example: posting to social media after a completed repair.
Does this save the business owner time?
If the answer is yes, it's probably worth building.
There's a quote I like:

"People don't buy software. They buy a better way of working."

That's what BennyFix should become.
I think we should build BennyFix with one guiding principle

Every feature should answer this question:

"Does this save someone time, make them money, or reduce their stress?"

If it does one of those three things, it's probably worth building.

For example:

Repair tracking → reduces stress.
Technician availability → saves time.
Social posting → saves time.
Inventory alerts → prevents lost sales.
Analytics → helps make money.
Automated follow-ups → improves customer retention.

That principle will help you decide what to build next when you have dozens of ideas.

If someone asked me what order I'd build BennyFix in now, it would be:
Core repairs (already well underway)
Technician ecosystem
Messaging & notifications
Studio (marketing)
Inventory
Invoices & payments
Reports & analytics
Customer loyalty & reviews

So here's our motto from now on:

Build for the next repair shop owner who opens BennyFix tomorrow.

If every decision makes their day a little easier, you're moving in the right direction.
