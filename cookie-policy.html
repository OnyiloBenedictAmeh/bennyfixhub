Welcome to Our Cookie Policy

BennyFix Hub values your privacy and believes you should understand how information is collected while you browse our website. This Cookie Policy explains what cookies are, why we use them, and how they contribute to providing a secure, efficient, and personalized experience on our platform.

By continuing to use BennyFix Hub, you acknowledge that cookies and similar technologies may be used in accordance with this Cookie Policy and our Privacy Policy.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your computer, smartphone, or other device when you visit a website. They help websites recognize your browser, remember your preferences, and improve your browsing experience over time.

Cookies do not usually contain information that directly identifies you. Instead, they allow websites to remember information such as your preferred language, login session, theme preferences, or other settings that make your next visit faster and more convenient.

BennyFix Hub may also use similar technologies such as local storage, session storage, or device identifiers where appropriate to support certain platform features.

2. How We Use Cookies

Cookies help BennyFix Hub deliver a smoother and more reliable experience. They allow us to remember your login session, maintain security, save your preferences, improve website performance, and better understand how visitors interact with different parts of our platform.

We also use cookies to help detect unusual activity, prevent fraudulent access attempts, and maintain the stability of our repair management services. Some cookies are necessary for the platform to function correctly, while others help us improve future versions of our services.

3. Types of Cookies We Use

BennyFix Hub uses different categories of cookies depending on their purpose. Essential cookies enable core platform functionality such as authentication, secure account access, and navigation. Without these cookies, many parts of the website would not function correctly.

Preference cookies remember settings that improve your experience, such as language selection, appearance preferences including light or dark mode, and other customization options that make the platform more convenient to use.

Performance and analytics cookies help us understand how visitors interact with our website by collecting anonymous usage statistics. This information helps us identify areas for improvement and optimize the overall user experience without unnecessarily identifying individual users.

4. Third-Party Cookies

Some features within BennyFix Hub may rely on trusted third-party services. These providers may set their own cookies or similar technologies when their services are integrated into our platform. Examples include payment processing, authentication services, analytics providers, cloud hosting, and embedded media where applicable.

BennyFix Hub does not control how third-party providers use their cookies. We encourage users to review the privacy and cookie policies of those providers to better understand how their information is collected and processed.

5. Managing Your Cookie Preferences

You have the ability to manage, restrict, or delete cookies through your web browser settings at any time. Most modern browsers allow you to view stored cookies, remove existing cookies, block future cookies, or receive notifications whenever a website attempts to store a cookie on your device.

Please note that disabling certain cookies may affect the functionality of BennyFix Hub. Features such as secure login, account management, repair tracking, saved preferences, and other personalized services may not operate as intended if essential cookies are disabled.

We recommend allowing essential cookies to remain enabled to ensure that the platform continues to function securely and efficiently.

6. Changes to This Cookie Policy

BennyFix Hub may update this Cookie Policy from time to time to reflect improvements to our services, changes in technology, updates to applicable laws, or changes in how cookies and similar technologies are used on our platform.

Whenever significant changes are made, the revised Cookie Policy will be published on this page together with the updated revision date. We encourage users to review this page periodically to remain informed about our use of cookies.

Your continued use of BennyFix Hub after updates have been published constitutes your acknowledgement of the revised Cookie Policy.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Cookie Policy or the technologies used by BennyFix Hub, we welcome you to contact our support team. We are committed to transparency and will make every reasonable effort to respond to your enquiries promptly.

Your trust is important to us, and we continuously work to ensure that your browsing experience remains secure, reliable, and respectful of your privacy.
