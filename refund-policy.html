Refund & Cancellation Policy

This Refund & Cancellation Policy explains how BennyFix Hub handles repair cancellations, payments, refunds, deposits, and related transactions. Our goal is to maintain a fair and transparent process for every customer while ensuring the sustainability of our repair services.

Welcome

BennyFix Hub is committed to providing honest pricing, professional repair services, and fair customer support. This Refund & Cancellation Policy explains the circumstances under which repairs may be cancelled, payments refunded, or service charges adjusted.

Because every repair is unique, the outcome of a request depends on several factors, including device condition, parts availability, labour already performed, and diagnostic findings. For this reason, refunds and cancellations are evaluated according to the principles outlined in this policy.

1. Repair Requests

When you submit a repair request through BennyFix Hub, our team or an assigned technician may begin reviewing the information you have provided in order to assess the reported fault. In some cases, additional diagnostics may be required before a final quotation or repair recommendation can be provided.

Submitting a repair request does not automatically guarantee that repair work will begin immediately. Certain repairs may require customer approval, advance payment, replacement parts, or additional technical assessment before work proceeds.

2. Payments and Deposits

Some repairs may require a deposit before work begins. Deposits help secure replacement parts, schedule technician time, and confirm your commitment to the repair process.

Any applicable charges, including labour, replacement components, diagnostics, taxes, or delivery fees, will be communicated before payment is requested whenever reasonably possible.

Payments made through BennyFix Hub are processed using trusted payment providers that apply industry-standard security measures to protect your financial information.

3. Repair Cancellation

Customers may request to cancel a repair before repair work has started. Where no labour has been performed and no replacement parts have been ordered specifically for the repair, cancellation may be approved without additional charges.

If repair work has already commenced or replacement parts have been purchased specifically for your device, BennyFix Hub may deduct reasonable costs already incurred before any remaining balance is refunded.

Cancellation requests should be submitted as soon as possible to minimize unnecessary costs and delays.

4. Refund Eligibility

BennyFix Hub aims to treat every refund request fairly and transparently. Refunds may be granted where a payment has been made in error, a repair cannot proceed due to circumstances within our reasonable control, or where applicable consumer protection laws require a refund.

Refund requests are reviewed individually because every repair is different. Factors such as work already completed, replacement parts purchased specifically for your device, diagnostic services already performed, and agreements made before the repair commenced may affect the amount that can be refunded.

Where a partial refund is appropriate, BennyFix Hub may deduct reasonable costs relating to labour already performed, diagnostic services completed, or non-returnable parts purchased specifically for your repair.

5. Digital Products and Services

BennyFix Hub may provide downloadable materials, educational resources, digital manuals, software-related services, or other digital products through the platform. Unless otherwise stated, payments for digital products that have already been delivered or accessed are generally non-refundable.

Where technical issues prevent delivery of a purchased digital product, we will make reasonable efforts to resolve the issue, provide an alternative means of access, or issue an appropriate refund where necessary.

6. Repairs That Cannot Be Completed

Although our technicians strive to repair every device successfully, some devices may be beyond economical or technical repair due to severe physical damage, liquid damage, component shortages, manufacturer restrictions, or previously undiscovered faults.

Where a repair cannot reasonably be completed, BennyFix Hub will explain the findings and discuss the available options with you. Depending on the circumstances, charges may still apply for approved diagnostic work or services already performed before the fault was determined to be beyond repair.

7. Payment Disputes

If you believe that a payment has been processed incorrectly or you have concerns regarding a transaction, we encourage you to contact BennyFix Hub before initiating a payment dispute with your financial institution. In many cases, issues can be resolved more quickly through direct communication.

We reserve the right to investigate disputed transactions and may request supporting information where necessary to verify the claim. Fraudulent or intentionally misleading payment disputes may result in the suspension of services or further action where permitted by law.

8. Exceptional Circumstances

BennyFix Hub understands that unexpected situations may arise. Where exceptional circumstances exist, we may consider alternative solutions that are fair to both the customer and our business. Such decisions are made on a case-by-case basis and should not be interpreted as creating a general entitlement to refunds outside the provisions of this policy.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may revise this Refund & Cancellation Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our services, operational procedures, legal requirements, or industry best practices. Updated versions will be published on this page together with the revised effective date.

Continued use of BennyFix Hub following the publication of an updated policy indicates your acceptance of the revised terms.

10. Contact Us

If you have questions regarding this Refund & Cancellation Policy or wish to discuss a refund or cancellation request, our support team is available to assist you. We are committed to handling every enquiry professionally, fairly, and with transparency.
