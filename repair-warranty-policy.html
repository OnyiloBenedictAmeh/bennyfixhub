Repair Warranty Policy

BennyFix Hub is committed to providing professional, reliable, and high-quality repair services. This Repair Warranty Policy explains the warranty that may apply to eligible repairs, replacement parts, and workmanship performed by our technicians. Our goal is to give every customer confidence in the services we provide while clearly defining the scope and limitations of our repair warranty.

Welcome

BennyFix Hub takes pride in delivering dependable repair services performed by qualified technicians using professional repair procedures. Our Repair Warranty Policy explains when warranty coverage applies, the responsibilities of both BennyFix Hub and our customers, and the circumstances under which warranty service may not be available.

Because every electronic device is different, warranty coverage may vary depending on the nature of the repair performed, the condition of the device before repair, and the replacement components used during the repair process.

1. Warranty Coverage

Subject to the terms of this policy, BennyFix Hub may provide a limited warranty covering defects directly related to the repair services or replacement parts supplied by our technicians. Where a repaired component fails because of workmanship or an eligible replacement part supplied during the repair, we will assess the device and determine the appropriate corrective action.

Depending on the findings of our inspection, warranty service may include additional repair work, replacement of the defective part, adjustment of the original repair, or another appropriate remedy at our discretion.

2. Warranty Period

The applicable warranty period begins on the date the repaired device is returned to the customer or made available for collection, unless otherwise stated in writing.

Warranty periods may vary depending on the type of repair, replacement components used, manufacturer recommendations, supplier warranty terms, or other service-specific conditions. Where a different warranty period applies, BennyFix Hub will communicate this clearly before or at the completion of the repair.

3. Replacement Parts

BennyFix Hub strives to use reliable replacement components sourced from trusted suppliers whenever possible. Depending on availability, repairs may involve original manufacturer parts, compatible replacement components, refurbished parts, or other suitable alternatives that meet the technical requirements of the repair.

The type of replacement component used may affect the warranty available for that repair. Where applicable, customers will be informed of any differences before the repair is completed.

4. Customer Responsibilities

Customers are responsible for using repaired devices with reasonable care following collection or delivery. We strongly recommend protecting repaired devices from impact, moisture, excessive heat, electrical damage, and other conditions that may affect their operation.

Customers should also inspect repaired devices as soon as reasonably possible after receiving them and notify BennyFix Hub if any concerns relating to the completed repair are identified during the applicable warranty period.

5. Warranty Exclusions

While BennyFix Hub is committed to delivering high-quality repairs, our warranty does not cover every circumstance that may affect a device after it has been repaired. Warranty coverage applies only to defects directly related to the repair work performed or eligible replacement parts supplied by BennyFix Hub.

Warranty coverage will generally not apply where a device has been subjected to accidental damage, liquid exposure, misuse, neglect, unauthorized repair attempts, improper handling, electrical surges, fire, natural disasters, or any modification carried out by another person after our repair has been completed.

The warranty also does not extend to faults that are unrelated to the original repair or to components that were already defective but not included as part of the approved repair service.

6. Making a Warranty Claim

If you believe a repaired device has developed a fault that is covered by this warranty, you should contact BennyFix Hub as soon as reasonably possible before attempting any further repairs. Prompt reporting allows our technicians to inspect the device and determine whether the issue falls within the scope of this warranty.

Customers may be asked to provide proof of repair, such as a repair receipt, invoice, service reference number, or any other information reasonably required to verify the original repair.

Devices submitted under warranty may undergo diagnostic testing before any repair decision is made. This helps confirm whether the reported issue is related to the original repair or another independent fault.

7. Warranty Limitations

This Repair Warranty represents the extent of BennyFix Hub's commitment regarding eligible repair work. Where warranty service is approved, our responsibility will generally be limited to repairing, adjusting, or replacing the affected component, or providing another appropriate remedy at our discretion.

To the fullest extent permitted by applicable law, BennyFix Hub shall not be responsible for indirect, incidental, or consequential losses arising from the use or inability to use a repaired device. This includes, but is not limited to, loss of data, loss of income, business interruption, or other damages that are not directly attributable to the repair work covered by this warranty.

8. Data Protection During Repairs

While every reasonable effort is made to protect customer devices during the repair process, customers remain responsible for backing up important files, photographs, contacts, applications, and other personal information before submitting a device for repair whenever possible.

Certain repair procedures may require software updates, operating system restoration, hardware replacement, or diagnostic processes that could affect stored data. BennyFix Hub cannot guarantee the preservation of user data during every repair and recommends that customers maintain independent backups of important information.

9. Changes to This Warranty Policy

BennyFix Hub may revise this Repair Warranty Policy from time to time to reflect improvements in our services, changes in supplier warranty programs, legal requirements, or operational procedures. Updated versions will be published on this page together with the revised effective date.

The version of this policy applicable to your repair will generally be the version in effect at the time your repair service was completed unless otherwise required by applicable law.

10. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Repair Warranty Policy or wish to submit a warranty claim, our support team is ready to assist you. We are committed to resolving warranty enquiries fairly, professionally, and as quickly as reasonably possible.
