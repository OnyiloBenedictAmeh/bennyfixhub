Welcome to BennyFix Hub

BennyFix Hub is committed to protecting your privacy and maintaining the trust you place in us whenever you use our services. Whether you are creating an account, requesting a repair, purchasing a device, contacting support, or simply browsing our website, we believe you deserve to know how your information is handled.

This Privacy Policy explains the types of information we collect, why we collect it, how we use it, and the choices you have regarding your personal information. By accessing or using BennyFix Hub, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

To provide our repair and support services effectively, BennyFix Hub collects certain information when you interact with our platform. This information helps us identify your account, process repair requests, communicate with you, and improve the quality of our services.

Depending on how you use the platform, the information we collect may include your name, email address, telephone number, profile picture, login credentials, and other details that you voluntarily provide when creating an account or contacting our support team.

When you submit a repair request, we also collect information relating to the device being repaired. This may include the device brand, model, serial number where applicable, description of the reported fault, uploaded photographs, repair progress, assigned technician, estimated repair cost, invoices, and repair history.

We may additionally collect technical information such as your IP address, browser type, operating system, device identifiers, pages visited, session activity, and general usage statistics. This helps us maintain platform security, troubleshoot technical issues, and improve your overall experience.

2. How We Use Your Information

The information collected through BennyFix Hub is used solely to deliver, maintain, and improve our services. It enables us to create and manage your account, process repair requests, assign qualified technicians, monitor repair progress, generate invoices, and notify you about important updates regarding your devices.

We also use your information to respond to customer enquiries, investigate technical issues, detect fraudulent activities, enhance the security of our platform, and develop new features that improve the overall experience for our users.

Where information is used for analytics, reporting, or product improvements, we strive to minimise the use of personally identifiable information wherever reasonably possible.

3. Marketing Communications

From time to time, BennyFix Hub may send you information relating to our services, promotional offers, product announcements, educational content, and special campaigns that we believe may be relevant to your interests.

Marketing communications are separate from important service-related notifications such as repair updates, payment confirmations, account security alerts, and system notices. These operational messages may still be sent where necessary for the delivery of our services.

Where applicable, you may choose to unsubscribe from promotional communications at any time without affecting your ability to continue using BennyFix Hub.

4. Artificial Intelligence Features

BennyFix Hub may use artificial intelligence technologies to assist with selected platform features, including generating repair summaries, creating marketing draft content, organising repair information, and improving customer support experiences.

AI-generated content is intended to assist our team and does not replace human review where accuracy is important. We continuously evaluate these features to ensure they operate responsibly and in a manner that respects user privacy.

We do not intentionally use AI to make automated decisions that have legal or similarly significant effects on our users without appropriate safeguards.

5. How We Share Your Information

BennyFix Hub respects your privacy and does not sell your personal information to third parties. Information is shared only where it is necessary to provide our services, fulfil legal obligations, protect our platform, or support legitimate business operations.

Your information may be shared with authorised repair technicians, secure payment providers, cloud hosting services, authentication providers, customer support tools, and other trusted partners whose services are required for the operation of BennyFix Hub.

We may also disclose information where required by applicable law, court order, or governmental authority, or where disclosure is necessary to protect the rights, safety, and security of BennyFix Hub, our users, or the public.

6. Data Security

Protecting your personal information is one of BennyFix Hub's highest priorities. We implement appropriate technical, administrative, and organisational measures designed to safeguard your information against unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration, misuse, or loss.

These measures may include secure authentication procedures, encrypted data transmission where appropriate, role-based access controls, continuous monitoring of our systems, and regular maintenance to improve the security of our platform.

While we work hard to protect the information entrusted to us, no internet-based service or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. For this reason, we encourage users to choose strong passwords, protect their login credentials, and notify us immediately if they believe their account has been compromised.

7. Data Retention

BennyFix Hub retains personal information only for as long as it is necessary to provide our services, maintain accurate repair records, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements.

The length of time that information is retained depends on the type of information involved and the purpose for which it was collected. Certain records relating to completed repairs, payments, and customer support interactions may be retained for legitimate business, accounting, or legal reasons.

When personal information is no longer required, we take reasonable steps to securely delete, anonymise, or otherwise dispose of it in a manner that helps protect your privacy.

8. Your Privacy Rights

We believe that you should have meaningful control over your personal information. Subject to applicable laws, you may request access to the information we hold about you, ask us to correct inaccurate information, update your account details, or request the deletion of personal information where appropriate.

You may also withdraw consent for certain optional forms of data processing or object to particular uses of your information where such rights are provided by law. Please note that some information may need to be retained where it is required to complete ongoing services, comply with legal obligations, prevent fraud, or protect the security of our platform.

Requests relating to your privacy can be submitted through our contact channels, and we will make reasonable efforts to respond within a reasonable period of time.

9. Cookies and Similar Technologies

BennyFix Hub uses cookies and similar technologies to improve the functionality, performance, and security of our website. These technologies help us remember your preferences, keep you signed in, understand how visitors interact with our platform, and enhance your browsing experience.

Some cookies are essential for the operation of the website, while others help us analyse usage trends and improve our services over time. You can choose to manage or disable cookies through your browser settings; however, doing so may affect the functionality of certain parts of the platform.

10. Children's Privacy

BennyFix Hub is intended for individuals who are legally permitted to use our services. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age required by applicable law without the involvement of a parent or legal guardian.

If we become aware that personal information has been collected from a child in a manner that does not comply with applicable requirements, we will take reasonable steps to remove the information from our systems as soon as practicable.

11. Third-Party Services

To provide certain features and services, BennyFix Hub may work with trusted third-party providers. These providers may assist with payment processing, cloud hosting, authentication, email delivery, analytics, file storage, customer support, and other essential platform operations.

Although we carefully select service providers that support secure business practices, their services are governed by their own privacy policies and terms of use. We encourage users to review those policies whenever they interact directly with third-party services.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

As BennyFix Hub continues to evolve, we may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect improvements to our services, changes in legal requirements, or updates to our business practices.

Whenever significant changes are made, the revised Privacy Policy will be published on this page together with an updated revision date. Continued use of our services after changes become effective indicates your acceptance of the revised Privacy Policy.

13. Contact Us

If you have questions, concerns, or requests relating to this Privacy Policy or the way your personal information is handled, we encourage you to contact BennyFix Hub through our official support channels.

We value transparency and will make reasonable efforts to respond to privacy-related enquiries promptly. Our goal is to ensure that every user feels confident about how their information is collected, used, and protected while using our platform.

14. Your Consent

By creating an account, accessing our website, requesting repairs, purchasing products, or otherwise using BennyFix Hub, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy and agree to the collection, use, storage, and disclosure of your information as described in this document.

If you do not agree with any part of this Privacy Policy, you should discontinue the use of our services. Your continued use of BennyFix Hub following any updates to this Privacy Policy constitutes your acceptance of those changes.

Your Privacy Matters

Thank you for trusting BennyFix Hub with your repair needs. We are committed to handling your information responsibly, maintaining transparency in our practices, and continuously improving the security of our platform so you can use our services with confidence.
