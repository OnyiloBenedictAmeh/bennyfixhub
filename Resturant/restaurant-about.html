Our story

A kitchen that started with Sunday lunch.

Hearth & Spice began as a weekly lunch for family and neighbours. The food is still the same: soups simmered for hours, party jollof with a smoky base, and fresh pepper ground every morning.

Cooked with patience, served with warmth.

Everything is made to order, so please give us a few minutes. We cook with honest ingredients, traditional flavours, and the kind of care that comes from feeding people you know.

Our menu draws on Yoruba and Igbo home cooking, with comfort dishes prepared for sharing, busy weekdays, and slow evenings on the patio.
